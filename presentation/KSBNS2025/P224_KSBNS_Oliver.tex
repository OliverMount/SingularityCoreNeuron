        \begin{block}{\LARGE Introduction}
        \justifying
        {\large
        1. Modeling \textbf{large-scale, biophysically detailed neural networks} increasingly relies on \textbf{high-performance computing (HPC)}. \\[0.5 cm]

        2. Deploying such simulations across diverse HPC environments is challenging due to \textbf{software incompatibilities} and \textbf{complex dependencies}. \\[0.5 cm]

        3. We address this using \textbf{Singularity containers} that encapsulate the entire environment, enabling portability and reproducibility without root privileges.\\[0.5 cm]

        4. Practical demonstrations: benchmarking HPC environments with \textbf{cerebellar mossy fiber$-$granule cell network} simulations.
        }
        \end{block}

        \begin{block}{\LARGE Containerization for Scientific Computing}
        \justifying
