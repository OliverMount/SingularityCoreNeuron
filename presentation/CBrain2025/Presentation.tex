            \begin{block}{\LARGE Introduction}
            \justifying
        {\large 1. Modeling \textbf{large-scale, biophysically detailed neural networks} increasingly relies on \textbf{high-performance computing (HPC)}. \\[0.5 cm]

        2. However, deploying simulations of such networks across diverse HPC environments remains challenging due to \textbf{software incompatibilities} and \textbf{complex dependencies}. \\[0.5 cm]

3. We address this issue using \textbf{Singularity containers} that encapsulats the entire simulation environment, enabling neuroscientists to achieve portability and reproducibility without requiring root privileges on HPC/Cloud systems.\\[0.5 cm]

4. We give practical demonstrations by benchmarking HPC environments with \textbf{cerebellar mossy fiber$-$granule cell network} simulations.
               }
            \end{block}
